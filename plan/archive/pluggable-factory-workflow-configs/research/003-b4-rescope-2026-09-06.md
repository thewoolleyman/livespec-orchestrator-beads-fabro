## What still holds from research 001 and 002

Every design decision the children were built on stands unchanged: a variant
is a complete directory and never an overlay; `implement-work-item` is the
reserved, undefinable name; precedence is the raw `--workflow` path, then the
recorded `--workflow-name`, then the item's `dispatch_workflow` pin, then
`dispatcher.default_workflow`, then the reserved name; no environment
variable selects a variant; every refusal fires before a Fabro run exists
with its own journal stage; and the registry keys are optional target-declared
capabilities in the class of `dispatcher.acp_nodes`, not
`RepoIntegrationContract` schema fields.

One correction to the record, found while resuming. The 11:52Z handoff on the
epic said that dispatch selection had accepted `bd-ib-27puvv` despite the
phantom claim left by reaped run `01M1V47W7Q39`, so no status reset was
needed. It had not. The earlier drives were refused by the green-master
preflight before selection ran at all, and once master was green the claim
check refused with "already claimed by a dispatch". The claim check keys on
`status == active` alone (`_dispatcher_readiness_diagnostics.py`), so an item
whose run was reaped from the factory stays claimed until something resets it.
`reconcile-runs` cannot do that reset, because the run no longer exists to
reconcile against. The remedy is the one the 10:50Z entry prescribed: verify no
live run, no branch, no pull request, then return the item to `ready`.

## The one design question, and why it is already answered

The console charter's phrase "with interview consent" reads as a Fabro run
that pauses to ask a human and resumes on the answer. This repository's v093,
`SPECIFICATION/contracts.md` section "A factory run never awaits a human",
forbids exactly that: the workflow "MUST NOT carry an interactive
human-decision node whose answer resumes the run", and the human's answer "is
expressed through ledger valves, never by attaching to a run". The console
session flagged the collision on 2026-09-06 as a cross-repo tension for the
maintainer and did not resolve it.

For variants registered in THIS repository the answer is not open. v093
governs, and the mechanism it requires shipped the same day: `bd-ib-uuohty`
made the `resolve-blocked` valve accept an answer, write it as a
poison-preflighted ledger comment before the transition, and journal it, and
the next dispatch's rendered goal already folds ledger comments in. So any
variant that needs consent is a two-phase run:

1. The propose phase does its work, writes its proposal, and terminates at a
   `needs_human` node carrying that proposal. The item rests at
   `blocked / needs-human`, which `needs-attention` already surfaces with the
   terminated run's question payload (`bd-ib-aqith2`).
2. A person, or later the consensus tier, answers through `resolve-blocked`.
   The answer is a ledger comment.
3. A second dispatch reads the answer from its goal and applies it.

"Interview consent" is therefore realized as the ledger answer. The scope
event records this as a rejection rather than a deferral: nothing proposes
retiring v093, and a variant that parks a run would be refused by the same
reconciliation that terminates parked runs after
`dispatcher.blocked_run_grace_seconds`.

## The four re-parented items, and where each goes

The sweep's one-line rationales were written before the registry existed and
before v093's answer route shipped. Re-read against both, the four items sort
into two spec-first carriers, one implementation follower, and one deferral.

### `bd-ib-e38q`: the spec carrier for the groom variant

This item asks that the "Grooming and slice-size calibration" section carry
livespec's resolved values call, which today it contradicts: the section still
says grooming stays human with no exception. The values call, from livespec's
archived `plan/spec-side-autonomy/research/brainstorm.md` under "Values
calls", item 2, reads that the groom cut may leave the maintainer's hands
"YES, but automated LAST and consensus-gated", only after Increments 1 and 2
land, only behind the same consensus tier, and only with slice-size ceilings
plus a regroom cap as required rails, for the cut only.

Its preconditions were checked on 2026-09-06 rather than assumed:

- Increments 1 and 2 have landed: `livespec-jvdvx4.3` and `livespec-jvdvx4.4`
  are closed in the livespec tenant, and the epic `livespec-jvdvx4` closed on
  2026-08-16.
- The consensus tier is NOT yet ratified in livespec core. Core's own spec
  says `consensus` "MUST escalate until the separately ratified consensus tier
  and its evidence are available", and the open core item `livespec-38bk`
  is the one binding consensus keys to a panel. Values-call item 3 said the
  tier's definition "deserves its own design pass before Increment 3".

Core resolved the same situation for its own Increment 3 by landing the
amendment in report-only shape: the automated decision is permitted in
principle and stays human-decided until the tier and its evidence exist. The
amendment `bd-ib-e38q` carries takes that shape, so the tier's later
ratification arms the automated cut without a second amendment here. The
mechanism the amendment names is the two-phase groom variant above.

The groom variant's implementation child is deliberately NOT filed by this
re-scope. Its contract is the text the revision lands, and the three children
filed on the morning of 2026-09-06 ahead of v099 each needed a
post-ratification rider correcting a scenario number and a reconciled
sentence. Filing after the revise pass costs one dispatch delay and removes
that whole class of drift.

### `bd-ib-ouoq`: the implementation follower

This item's first criterion asked for a resolution to be chosen and recorded
before any code, among three candidates: (a) a required approval record that
the function stamps and refuses without, (b) an unsynthesisable approval token,
(c) recording whatever evidence is supplied without judging it. The scope
event chooses (a). In the groom-variant path the approval record is supplied
mechanically, as the `resolve-blocked` answer comment's invoker and comment id,
so (b)'s token protects against a synthesis that the answer route already
prevents, and (c) records without refusing, which the near-miss in the item's
own description shows is too weak. The choice is recorded for objection; the
item is blocked on `bd-ib-e38q`'s revision and stays at `backlog` until it is
on master, so the objection window is real.

### `bd-ib-vlhp`: the spec carrier for a per-item merge hold

The sweep called the merge hold "a typed variant input". Read precisely, it is
a per-ITEM input, and v092's `merge_mode` field is per-REPO. Three routes were
weighed:

| Route | Spec change | Per-item | Drift risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A second variant shipped inside the plugin bundle whose pr prompt omits the auto-merge arm | yes, lifts a standing deferral so the registry resolves bundle-relative entries | yes, via the `dispatch_workflow` pin | a near-copy of the reserved workflow maintained beside it | rejected |
| A per-repo committed hold-merge variant, registered under `dispatcher.workflows` | no | yes, via the pin | every repo that wants one hold copies the whole workflow | stopgap only |
| A typed integration input, declared in the item's ledger metadata and rendered by the Dispatcher beside `merge_mode`, honored by the pr stage | yes, one field on the ratified input set | yes, natively | none; one workflow, one input | chosen |

The chosen route routes `bd-ib-vlhp` to `propose-change`. The consumer is
`livespec-console-beads-fabro-ag0`, whose merge the maintainer ruled to hold on
2026-08-21 and which has waited since for want of this capability. The second
route is recorded on the item as the stopgap the console tenant can use today,
because the registry that makes it possible landed this morning.

### `bd-ib-js1f`: deferred as a duty of the groom variant

This item's own self-correction, added hours after filing, found zero stale
items in a seeded random sample of ten against four of eight in one hot area,
and retitled the item accordingly. A standalone sweep mechanism is not
warranted by that evidence; a sweep of recently-worked areas is a natural duty
of a variant that already reads those items to groom them. It stays parked
under this epic until the groom variant child is filed, with its rider saying
so and asking that it not be dispatched.

## The deferrals, with their reconsideration points

- Revise, gap-capture and spec-doctor as workflow variants: deferred until a
  consumer files a need. The console plan's exit criterion was consuming b1
  through b3, and no item anywhere names which spec-side operation needs to be
  a factory run; every one of them runs in-session through the livespec Driver
  today. When one is filed it will be a two-phase v093 run like groom.
- Panel and review variants, and any "panel policy enumeration": deferred until
  core ratifies the consensus tier. Nothing in this repository's contract
  defines a panel policy (measured: zero matches for "panel" in
  `SPECIFICATION/contracts.md`), so there is nothing to build a variant
  against yet.
- A second variant shipped inside the plugin bundle: still deferred from the
  morning scope event. The drift signal that would justify hoisting one is two
  or more fleet repositories committing the same variant directory.
