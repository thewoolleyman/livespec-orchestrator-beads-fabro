## Lesson

A hold stops new launches only. A launch takes about 8 minutes from LAUNCH to a listed
run, so any launch started in the 8 minutes before the hold lands on the board after it.
Before cancelling for a restart, read the in-flight launches (fleet in-flight markers, or
launcher logs with a LAUNCH line and no listed run) or ask the dispatcher for them.
