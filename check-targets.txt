# Each recipe was run alone, in sequence, capped at 90 s, on vps (vmi3006760,

# 45 s or more, or hit the cap, is `slow` and is deferred on the expedited
# path; 45 s leaves a 25% margin under the hooks' 60 s per-gate bound. A member
# whose first time was slow but within reach of the threshold was re-timed
# alone at lower load (38-45), and the faster run classifies it: `slow` defers the
# gate, while a fast gate that overruns 60 s is still recorded as a deferred
# timeout, so the faster reading is the safer one. A gate that encodes a
# normal-path precondition an incident commit cannot meet by design is
# `not-applicable` and names that precondition. Both `slow` and `not-applicable`
# gates are recorded as DEFERRED, with their reason, in every expedited
# receipt, and both still run in the full `just check` aggregate.
